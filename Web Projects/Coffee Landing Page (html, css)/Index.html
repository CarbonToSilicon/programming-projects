About Garden Coffee Cafe


Welcome to Garden Coffee Cafe, where the freshness of nature meets the ultimate coffee experience. We believe a great cup of coffee should be pure, refreshing, and full of life. That is why we bring nature's finest flavors straight from the lush plantations to your cup.

Our Story


Our journey is rooted in a deep love for authentic coffee and a commitment to quality. We source our premium coffee beans directly from the rich, green hills of Kerala. By partnering with local estates, we bring you the authentic taste of South India's finest crop, expertly roasted to unlock its rich aroma and smooth flavor.

Why Choose Us?


  • Straight from Kerala: We source our beans directly from the best coffee-growing regions of Kerala for unmatched authenticity.

  • All Types of Coffee: Whether you love a strong Espresso, a creamy Latte, or a refreshing Cold Brew, we serve every style to match your mood.

  • Pure & Fresh: Every cup is crafted with care, ensuring you get the freshest taste in every single sip.
